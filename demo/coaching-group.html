Curriculum Breakdown

The 4-Week Reset Roadmap

Week 1

Unpacking Societal Scripts & Expectations

We deconstruct internal pressure and external noise regarding timelines, age, and relationship status. You'll redefine what happiness looks like on your own terms.

Week 2

Building Unshakable Self-Worth

Shift from seeking external validation to cultivating deep inner trust. Learn practical daily routines that protect your mental energy and elevate self-esteem.

Week 3

Mastering Boundaries & Solo Confidence

Say goodbye to people-pleasing. Learn script formulas to set firm boundaries with friends, family, and dating apps—plus embrace doing things solo with total ease.

Week 4

Designing Your Radiant Life Vision

Craft a tangible 90-day action plan for your travel, career, personal goals, and community connection. Graduate with complete clarity on your next steps.
