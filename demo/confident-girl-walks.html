Confident Girl Walks

Free women’s walks throughout Fresno and the Central Valley created for connection, friendship, and community. Come walk, talk and connect with kind, supportive women looking to build community. Whether you come alone or bring a friend, you’ll always have someone to walk with. You never have to walk alone.
