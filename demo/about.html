Meet Your Coach

Hi, I'm Marty Ortiz.
I've been where you are.

As a Co-Active Certified Professional Coach based in Fresno, California, I help women transform singlehood and life transitions into their most empowered chapters.

For years, I navigated the noise of societal expectations, feeling stuck, disconnected, and unsure of my next steps. When I discovered the power of intentional self-love and clarity, everything changed. Now, I dedicate my practice—Marty Ortiz Confidence Coach—to guiding women back to themselves.

My Philosophy

Singleness is Not a Problem to Be Solved

We live in a culture that often treats singleness as an awkward interim period. I believe it is one of the most fertile seasons of your entire life.

Through my confidence coaching, my goal is to give you the practical mindset shifts, self-trust tools, and accountability needed to live boldly, travel, build a thriving career, and step into every room with unshakable confidence.

  • ✨ Self-Discovery: Clarify who you are outside of relationships or expectations.
  • ✨ Boundaries & Courage: Learn to say no to people-pleasing and yes to yourself.
  • ✨ Actionable Steps: Turn insights into real daily habits and milestones.
