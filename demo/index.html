Nice to meet you

Hi, I’m Marty.

I’m a Co-Active Certified Professional Coach and women’s confidence coach helping women build confidence, trust themselves, and create a life they genuinely love.

I know what it feels like to question your worth and look outside yourself for the things you think will finally make you feel happy or complete. I also know what can happen when you stop doing that and start choosing yourself.

That’s why I do this work.

Through coaching, community, and real-life experiences, I help women see themselves differently, believe in who they are, and build the confidence to live life on their own terms.
