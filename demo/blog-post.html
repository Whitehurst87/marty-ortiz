Why Being Single Isn't a Waiting Room (And How to Own Your Season)

By Marty Ortiz, CPCC • Published July 15, 2026 • 5 min read

How often have you caught yourself thinking, "I'll book that trip once I have a partner," or "I'll buy nice home decor when I settle down"?

In our culture, singleness is frequently framed as an interim state—a holding pattern while you wait for the "real" story of your life to begin. But as a Co-Active Certified Coach, I want to invite you to shift that perspective completely.

1. Reframe Singleness as Your Launchpad

Your single years are not a waiting room; they are your launchpad. This is the era where you get to discover your unvarnished preferences, cultivate financial independence, and make decisions without needing to compromise.

2. Practice Small Acts of Solo Courage

Building confidence doesn't happen overnight. It is built through small, intentional acts of self-trust: taking yourself out to coffee without pulling out your phone, booking a weekend trip to a nearby city, or trying a new workout class solo.

3. Build Your Support System In Real Life

Solitude should never mean isolation. Connection is essential to human happiness. That is why community groups like Confident Girl Walks exist—to give you a safe, welcoming space to connect with other women who are also dedicated to personal growth.
